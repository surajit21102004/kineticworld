# Support & Maintenance Plan: KineticWorld (Stage 19)

## Support Window & SLA
- **Primary Support Contact**: `contact@kineticworld.ai`
- **Bug Severity Definition**:
  - **P1 (Critical)**: Lead capture API offline or website failing to load. (SLA: 4 hours)
  - **P2 (Major)**: Interactive component glitch (e.g., ROI slider or AI Playground formatting issue). (SLA: 24 hours)
  - **P3 (Minor)**: Minor typography or alignment tweak. (SLA: 48 hours)
